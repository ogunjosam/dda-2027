Areas of interest

Topics

DDA 2027 spans the breadth of nonlinear science, from foundational theory to applications in the climate, life, and engineered systems.

Core areas

Chaos theory Chaos synchronization Nonlinear time series Fractal theory Vibrations and Resonances Bifurcation theory Pattern formation Complex networks

Applications

Climate dynamics Space physics Biophysics Neuroscience Econophysics Epidemics Ecology Materials science Mathematics and statistics

Computational and methodological

Artificial Intelligence and Machine learning Deep learning Computational methods Scientific computing Reduced-order modelling Numerical analysis

Physical and engineered systems

Electronic chaos Electronic implementation Control theory Fluid mechanics Turbulence Statistical physics Stochastic systems

Topic areas for this year's meeting include nonlinear time series in climate and space physics, econophysics, biophysics, neuroscience, network dynamics, machine learning, computational methods, synchronization, stochastic resonance, fractal theory, and electronic implementation. Contributions in adjacent areas of nonlinear science are also welcome.
