Location

Redeemer's University

Ede, Osun State, Nigeria.

DDA 2027 will be hosted at Redeemer's University, located in Ede, Osun State, in south-western Nigeria. The university offers a modern academic campus with dedicated conference facilities, ideally suited to accommodate plenary sessions, parallel talks, poster displays, and social events.

Getting there

The nearest major international airports are Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Lagos and Ibadan Airport, both with onward road connections to Ede. Detailed travel guidance and airport-transfer options will be published closer to the conference date.

Accommodation

A block of hotel rooms will be reserved for conference participants at negotiated rates. Reservation details will be shared with registered attendees.

Visa

Participants requiring an entry visa to Nigeria may request an official letter of invitation from the local organizing committee upon registration.
