About the conference

Where the Science of complexity converges in the African continent.

Dynamics Days is a series of major interdisciplinary international conferences on the theory and application of chaos and nonlinear dynamics, hosted for decades in cities across Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

DDA 2027 is the inaugural African edition, bringing together researchers in physics, mathematics, biology, engineering, and the geosciences to advance nonlinear science on the continent where it has long been under-represented, and to open new pathways for African researchers to join the global conversation.

The conference will host plenary talks, minisymposia, contributed talks, and posters, with proceedings published by a leading international publisher.
