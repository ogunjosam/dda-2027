Contributions

Submissions and publications

Guidelines for submitting your work to DDA 2027 and information on conference proceedings.

Submission portal

All submissions are made via the online conference portal at www.submithere.org. Contributions accepted include:

  • Invited talks (up to 6 pages)
  • Contributed talks (up to 4 pages)
  • Poster presentations
  • Minisymposium proposals

Templates

Submissions must use the official conference Word or LaTeX template. Templates will be available for download from the submission portal.

Review process

All contributions undergo peer review by the technical committee. Authors will be notified of acceptance decisions ahead of the registration deadline.

Proceedings

Peer-reviewed publication

Accepted contributions will be published in conference proceedings by one of:

Springer IOP IEEE AIP

Final publisher will be confirmed closer to the conference date.
